    How the points work
    • A win is worth 50, 47, 44 or 41 points depending on whether the opponent is a 1st, 2nd, 3rd or 4th division team. A loss is worth 39, 36, 33 or 30.
    • Division comes from the opponent's win percentage: 75% or better is 1st, 50% to 75% is 2nd, 25% to 50% is 3rd, under 25% is 4th.
    • Opponents one class above D1 add 2 points, two classes above add 3. Smaller classes never subtract.
    • Average is total points divided by matches played. Divisions use opponents' current records, so Riverside's total can shift as opponents finish their seasons.
    • Ties in average go to head-to-head, then most 1st division opponents, then opponents' combined win percentage.
